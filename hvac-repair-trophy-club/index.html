How to prepare for a repair visit

The technician will need to assess the system and discuss what they find. Be ready to explain whether the issue affects heating, cooling, or both, and whether it happens all the time or only sometimes. If you know when the equipment was last serviced, that may also be useful to share.

Use this quick preparation list:
- Write down the start date and any patterns you've noticed.
- Identify the rooms or areas where you feel a change.
- Keep the route to the equipment clear.
- Collect any service notes or details you already have.

Avoid repeatedly restarting a system that isn't working normally or trying repairs beyond simple, safe checks. If you notice something that could be unsafe, step away from the equipment and call for help. Don't wait to call just because you can't name the failed part.
